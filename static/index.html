Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workday, Workable, Recruitee, Breezy, Oracle (…oraclecloud.com), Eightfold, Keka (…keka.com/careers) and amazon.jobs are read directly. Most other careers sites (iCIMS, SuccessFactors, Phenom…) work through their public job feed or the job data they publish for Google. Those sites are slower to crawl.
